# 🎲 GO Game-On! Symposium 2027 — Wits Hybrid Conference Platform (`EventHub`)

**Official Hybrid Conference & Symposium Web Platform** for the **GO Game-On! Symposium 2027** (**11–12 March 2027**), hosted by **University of the Witwatersrand (Wits)**, **School of Therapeutic Sciences**, and **eFundanathi eZone ("Learn with us")** at the Wits Education Campus & Online.

- **Umbrella University Platform Name**: **EventHub · Wits Event Cloud** (`eventhub.wits.ac.za`)
- **Symposium Subdomain Route**: `gameon2027.eventhub.wits.ac.za`
- **Official Theme**: *"Playing Our Stories: Educational, Digital and Traditional Games for Learning, Teaching and Community"*
- **Format**: Hybrid Event (In-Person at eFundanathi eZone, Wits Education Campus + Online via Zoom & Microsoft Teams)

---

## ✨ Key Platform Modules (Whova + Google Drive Folder Routing + Peer Review + Gamification)

1. **🏠 Overview, Save-the-Date & 4 Official Tracks**:
   - Centered **GO Game-On! Symposium 2027** vector logo on top, followed by the centered **✨ SAVE THE DATE & CALL FOR ABSTRACTS** badge.
   - Host banner with *"Hosted by Wits University, School of Therapeutic Sciences & eFundanathi eZone ('Learn with us')"* on the left and the official *"your host"* logos badge on the right.
   - Live countdown timer to **11 March 2027**, Call for Abstracts milestone cards, and interactive **Tracks 1–4** topic pills.
2. **☁️ Google Drive Multi-Folder Upload Routing**:
   - Every file upload across the platform automatically routes into its dedicated Google Drive subfolder under `/EventHub_GameOn_2027/`:
     - `01_Abstracts/Track_1_Playful_Pedagogies/`
     - `01_Abstracts/Track_2_Simulation_XR_AI/`
     - `01_Abstracts/Track_3_Games_Health_Society/`
     - `01_Abstracts/Track_4_Research_Partnerships/`
     - `02_Speaker_Presentations_&_Slide_Decks/`
     - `03_Speaker_&_Participant_Bios_&_Photos/`
     - `04_Playable_Game_Builds_&_Rulebooks/`
     - `05_Sponsor_Logos_&_Partner_Brochures/`
3. **📅 Interactive Agenda & Personal Calendar Manager**:
   - Filter by Day 1 / Day 2, Track 1–4, or **"My Saved Agenda"**, with **1-Click `.ICS` Calendar Export**.
4. **🎓 Speakers, Inter-University Directory & Bio Builder**:
   - Showcases speakers and participants across **Wits, UCT, UP, UJ, Stellenbosch, UKZN**, and partner institutions with bio & slide deck uploads.
5. **⚖️ Academic Peer Review & Scientific Committee Workspace**:
   - 4-criterion academic evaluation rubric (1–5 scale) + **1-Click Promote Accepted Abstract to Calendar**.
6. **📡 Hybrid Live Stream Stage (Zoom & Microsoft Teams)**:
   - Multi-stage switcher (eZone Main Auditorium on Zoom, Therapeutic Sciences XR Lab on MS Teams, Playtest Commons on Zoom), upvotable Live Q&A, and live audience polls.
7. **🏆 Wits Education Campus Amazing Race & Scavenger Hunt**:
   - Playable 5-leg campus & online puzzle trail, QR Scavenger Hunt code redeemer, and live **Inter-University Leaderboard**.
8. **🤝 Sponsors Widget Slider & Official Contact Footer**:
   - Interactive auto-scrolling **Sponsors & Partner Placeholders Carousel** + full footer with official contact emails (`wits.ezone@gmail.com` and `efundanathi.ezone@wits.ac.za`) and quick message form.
