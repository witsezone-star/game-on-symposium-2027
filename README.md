# 🎲 GO Game-On! Symposium 2027 — Wits Hybrid Conference Platform (`ImbizoHub`)

**Official Hybrid Conference & Symposium Web Platform** for the **GO Game-On! Symposium 2027** (**11–12 March 2027**), hosted by **University of the Witwatersrand (Wits)**, **School of Therapeutic Sciences**, and **eFundanathi eZone ("Learn with us")** at the Wits Education Campus & Online.

- **Umbrella University Platform Name**: **ImbizoHub · Wits Hybrid Event Cloud** (`imbizo.wits.ac.za`)
- **Symposium Subdomain Route**: `gameon2027.imbizo.wits.ac.za`
- **Official Theme**: *"Playing Our Stories: Educational, Digital and Traditional Games for Learning, Teaching and Community"*
- **Format**: Hybrid Event (In-Person at eFundanathi eZone, Wits Education Campus + Online via Zoom & Microsoft Teams)

---

## ✨ Key Platform Modules (Whova + Academic Peer Review + Gamification)

1. **🏠 Overview, Save-the-Date & 4 Official Tracks**:
   - Built directly from the official `GO Symposium 2017 SDT [Recovered].ai` and `GO Symposium 2017 Abstracts.ai` vector artwork.
   - Live countdown timer to **11 March 2027**, Call for Abstracts milestone cards, and interactive **Tracks 1–4** topic pills.
2. **📅 Interactive Agenda & Personal Calendar Manager**:
   - Filter by Day 1 / Day 2, Track 1–4, hybrid format, or **"My Saved Agenda"**.
   - **1-Click `.ICS` Calendar Export** compatible with Microsoft Outlook, Google Calendar, and Apple Calendar.
3. **🎓 Speakers, Inter-University Directory & Bio Builder**:
   - Showcases speakers and participants across **Wits, UCT, UP, UJ, Stellenbosch, UKZN**, and partner institutions.
   - **Interactive Speaker & Participant Bio Creator** with presentation slide deck upload and Whova-style meetup networking invites.
4. **📄 Call for Abstracts & Presentation Upload Portal**:
   - Structured abstract submission mapped to **Tracks 1–4** and official sub-topics, with slide deck / poster / game build upload.
5. **⚖️ Academic Peer Review & Scientific Committee Workspace**:
   - 4-criterion academic evaluation rubric (1–5 scale) + **1-Click Promote Accepted Abstract to Calendar**.
6. **📡 Hybrid Live Stream Stage (Zoom & Microsoft Teams)**:
   - Multi-stage switcher (eZone Main Auditorium on Zoom, Therapeutic Sciences XR Lab on MS Teams, Playtest Commons on Zoom), custom stream URL configurator, upvotable Live Q&A, and live audience polls.
7. **🏆 Wits Education Campus Amazing Race & Scavenger Hunt**:
   - Playable 5-leg campus & online puzzle trail, QR Scavenger Hunt code redeemer (`WITSEZONE`, `MORABARABA27`, `UBUNTUNETWORK`, `PARTNER2027`, `PLAYOURSTORIES`), and live **Inter-University Leaderboard**.
8. **🤝 University Partnerships, Sponsorship Tiers & Virtual Booths**:
   - Tiered sponsorship packages (Platinum, Gold, Silver, Community/Grant Partner), interactive exhibitor booths with passport stamping, and partnership pledge form.
